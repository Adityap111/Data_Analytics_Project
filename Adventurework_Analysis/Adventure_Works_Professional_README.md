# Adventure Works Data Analytics Project

> End-to-end business analytics project using \*\*SQL, Microsoft Excel, Power BI, and Tableau\*\* to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product performance.

\---

## 📌 Project Overview

Adventure Works Cycles is a multinational bicycle manufacturing and sales company operating across **North America, Europe, and Asia**.

This project analyzes Adventure Works business data to identify actionable insights that can support better decision-making around:

* Sales performance
* Customer behavior
* Product performance

The project combines data preparation, SQL analysis, dashboard development, visualization, and business storytelling across multiple analytics tools.

\---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Adventure Works wanted to use its business data to:

1. Identify high-value customers
2. Improve sales performance
3. Optimize inventory
4. Reduce operational costs
5. Expand its online presence

The analysis was organized around four key focus areas:

**Sales Performance → Customer Behavior → Product Analysis**

## 📊 Analysis Areas

### 1\. Sales Performance Analysis

The sales analysis evaluates overall revenue, profitability, regional performance, and monthly trends.

**Key findings:**

* **Total Sales:** ₹29.36M
* **Profit Margin:** 41.15%
* **North America and Australia** are the top contributing regions.
* Monthly trends show a steady increase in sales and profit margin.

\---

### 2\. Customer Behaviour Analysis

The customer analysis focuses on customer base characteristics, sales contribution, and repeat purchasing behavior.

**Key findings:**

* Approximately **18K customers** were analyzed.
* Customer distribution shows a balanced gender mix.
* **Adult customers** generate the highest sales across income levels.
* **Repeat purchase rate:** 86.5%, indicating strong customer loyalty.

\---

### 3\. Inventory \& Product Analysis

The product analysis evaluates category-level and individual-product performance.

**Key findings:**

* **Bikes** contribute the highest share of total sales and product demand across regions.
* The **Mountain-200** series is the strongest individual product line by revenue.
* **Accessories** deliver strong profit margins despite comparatively lower sales volume.

\---

### 4\. SQL Analysis

SQL was used to combine and analyze sales, customer, and product information.

The analysis includes:

* Table joins
* Aggregate functions such as `SUM`, `COUNT`, and `AVG`
* Filtering with `WHERE`
* Grouping with `GROUP BY`
* Filtering aggregated results with `HAVING`
* Subqueries
* KPI calculations

The SQL analysis was used to derive insights across sales performance, customer behavior, inventory/product performance, and operational efficiency.

* Prioritize inventory planning around high-demand bicycle products.
* Leverage the strong profitability of accessories through appropriate sales strategies.
* Monitor monthly sales and profitability trends to support business planning.
